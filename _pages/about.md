I am a robotics and aerospace engineer with expertise in mechatronic design, embedded systems,
sensor integration, and autonomous robotic navigation. I obtained my PhD *cum laude* in
Electrical, Electronics and Communication Engineering at the [Polytechnic of Turin](https://www.polito.it/),
researching mobility and state estimation for planetary exploration rovers at the
[Interdepartmental Centre for Service Robotics (PIC4SeR)](https://pic4ser.polito.it/), under the
supervision of [Prof. Marcello Chiaberge](https://pic4ser.polito.it/people/marcello-chiaberge/).

My research focuses on **autonomous robot navigation in extreme and perception-degraded
environments**, particularly planetary surfaces and natural underground environments. Key
contributions include:

- A **state-estimation framework for a four-wheeled lunar rover** with active articulated
  suspensions, enabling three-dimensional motion estimation over uneven terrain through
  multimodal sensor fusion ([Acta Astronautica, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actaastro.2026.02.010)).
- **CAVERS**, a multimodal SLAM dataset collected in a natural karstic cave with
  millimetre-accurate motion-capture ground truth, addressing the scarcity of benchmark
  datasets for SLAM in natural subterranean environments
  ([IEEE RA-L, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2026.3726391)) — openly released
  [on Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19367714).
- The mechatronic design of a **4-DOF robotic arm for terrain sampling** on exploration rovers,
  validated for planetary surface operations
  ([IEEE MetroAeroSpace, 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/metroaerospace57412.2023.10190044)).

During my Master's studies I designed and characterised a 4-DOF robotic arm for terrain
sampling on exploration rovers (IEEE MetroAeroSpace 2023, first author). At the
[German Aerospace Center (DLR)](https://www.dlr.de/en/rm), I contributed to the
[ARCHES](https://arches-project.de/) multi-robot planetary exploration campaign on Mount
Etna, supporting the experimental validation of autonomous multi-robot capabilities in
planetary analogue environments. During my PhD, I undertook an industrial secondment at
[Thales Alenia Space Italy](https://www.thalesaleniaspace.com/) within the Italian Space
Agency's *Universal Locomotion System* programme, developing sensor fusion and localization
for GNSS-denied lunar rover navigation, together with avionics integration, the rover's
Electrical Power System, and embedded control and telemetry firmware. At the
[Space Robotics Lab](https://www.uma.es/) of the University of Málaga, I investigated
thermal-visual localization in subterranean environments.

I am committed to **open science**: the CAVERS dataset and its software are openly released on
[Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19367714) (CC BY 4.0) and
[GitHub](https://github.com/spaceuma/cavers), and have already been reused by third parties,
including a public [Voxel51 tutorial](https://voxel51.com/) on multimodal robotic-data
workflows. I create and maintain open-source software such as
[covariance_geometry_ros](https://github.com/giafranchini/covariance_geometry_ros) (ROS 2,
Apache-2.0) and co-developed a Unity-based virtual reality framework for
[lunar exploration](https://github.com/PIC4SeR/vr_lunar_exploration) (MIT).

My toolset spans perception, state-estimation and navigation software in modern C++ and Python
(ROS 2, OpenCV, PyTorch), embedded software in C with real-time operating systems (FreeRTOS),
and mechatronic design with mechanical CAD, FEA and prototyping. I am a native Italian speaker,
fluent in English (C1) and Spanish (B2).
