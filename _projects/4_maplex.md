**MAPLE-X** (*MArtian Padua student manipulator for soiL EXtraction*) was a student project
funded by the University of Padua, part of the MORPHEUS student rover team. I conceived,
designed and controlled a **4-DOF robotic arm for terrain sample collection**, integrated into
the MORPHEUS rover, covering mechatronic design, control software development and ROS 2
integration.

The work constituted my Master's thesis and was published at the
[IEEE MetroAeroSpace 2023](https://doi.org/10.1109/metroaerospace57412.2023.10190044)
workshop, demonstrating the arm's suitability for planetary surface operations.
