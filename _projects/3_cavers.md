**CAVERS** is a multimodal dataset collected in a natural karstic cave, addressing the scarcity
of benchmark datasets for SLAM in natural subterranean environments. It was developed at the
[Space Robotics Lab](https://www.uma.es/) of the University of Málaga during my visiting
research period, investigating thermal-visual localization under degraded illumination.

The dataset comprises **24 sequences (~335 GB)** of RGB-D, thermal, LiDAR and IMU data with
**millimetre-accurate motion-capture ground truth**, openly released on
[Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19367714) under CC BY 4.0. The accompanying software is
available on [GitHub](https://github.com/spaceuma/cavers).

The dataset has already been reused by third parties, including a public
[Voxel51 tutorial](https://voxel51.com/) on multimodal robotic-data workflows.
