The **Universal Locomotion System (ULS)** is a lunar rover demonstrator funded by the Italian
Space Agency (ASI) under the call *Tecnologie Abilitanti Robotiche e di Intelligenza
Artificiale*, with Thales Alenia Space Italy as prime contractor and PIC4SeR (Polytechnic of
Turin) among the consortium partners. The project concluded with a successful Final Review,
with the ETNA rover demonstrator validated on Mount Etna.

**My contribution** (industrial secondment at Thales Alenia Space Italy, 2023–2024):

- Development of a multimodal state-estimation framework for the ULS rover: sensor fusion of
  onboard IMU, stereo camera, time-of-flight cameras and custom wheel-odometry measurements,
  integrated in the rover autonomous navigation stack for GNSS-denied lunar navigation.
- Design of the rover Electrical Power System (EPS) and development of its embedded control
  and telemetry firmware.
- Implementation of a Zenoh-based communication interface over TCP/IP between the EPS
  microcontroller and the rover's onboard computer.
