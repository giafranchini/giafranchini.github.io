**ARCHES** (*Autonomous Robotic Networks to Help Modern Societies*) is a Helmholtz Association
project led by the DLR Institute of Robotics and Mechatronics, with partners including the
Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, the Alfred Wegener Institute, GEOMAR and ESA participation.
The programme culminated in a space-analogue demonstration mission on Mount Etna, validating
autonomous multi-robot capabilities in a planetary analogue environment.

As a **research intern at DLR** (2022), I developed high-level autonomy skills for multi-robot
planetary exploration and mobile manipulation, based on RAFCON hierarchical state machines,
and directly participated in the demonstration campaign on Mount Etna.
