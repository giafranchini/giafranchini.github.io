I am committed to open science and reusable research software:

- **covariance_geometry_ros** ([GitHub](https://github.com/giafranchini/covariance_geometry_ros)):
  creator and maintainer of a ROS 2 package for pose and covariance composition
  (Apache-2.0 licence, since 2023).
- **CAVERS software** ([GitHub](https://github.com/spaceuma/cavers)): tooling for the CAVERS
  multimodal SLAM dataset, released as supplementary material of the RA-L 2026 paper
  (dataset: [Zenodo, CC BY 4.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19367714)).
- **vr_lunar_exploration** ([GitHub](https://github.com/PIC4SeR/vr_lunar_exploration)):
  co-developer of a Unity virtual-reality framework for human-robot collaboration in lunar
  missions, developed at PIC4SeR (MIT licence, 2024), released as supplementary material of
  the IAC 2024 oral presentation.

The CAVERS dataset has already been reused by third parties, including a public Voxel51
tutorial on multimodal robotic-data workflows.
