## Teaching role

During the academic year 2024/2025, I held a teaching contract at the
[Polytechnic of Turin](https://www.polito.it/) for the **Electronic Systems for Mechatronics**
module of the MSc in Mechatronic Engineering. I delivered 20 hours of lectures and exercises,
and participated in examinations and student tutoring.

## Thesis co-supervision

I co-supervised two Master's theses at the
[Interdepartmental Centre for Service Robotics (PIC4SeR)](https://pic4ser.polito.it/):

- *Terrain traversability analysis for planetary rovers* (with Andrea Chiuchiarelli).
- *Payload dispensing for lunar exploration rovers*.
